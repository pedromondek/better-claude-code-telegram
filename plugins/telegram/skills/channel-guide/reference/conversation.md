# Decision questions and reports on Telegram

The user reads only the message: everything needed to decide goes inside it.

## Before asking

- Look for what is already decided (the decision records of the project, the memory, this conversation) and ask only what is still open.
- A rule you deduced from what the user did becomes a question to them, never a rule you start applying.

## The question

- The recommended option comes first, marked as recommended.
- Every option carries a concrete example of its result: the name, the command, the text or the screen it produces.
- A decision shows its research: the path you took, what you measured and why each discarded option left.
- One pending item per message, with the context before the question.

## Reports

- At the end of a long stage, a short summary: what finished, what you found and what comes next. A decision that follows goes in the same message, with its buttons.
- Name a task by its number and its title (or what it does), never by the number alone.
